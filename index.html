Welcome to the official website of Tanmay Hardia. Tanmay Hardia is an emerging singer and songwriter who creates emotional and meaningful music. His songs focus on deep lyrics, storytelling, and melodies that connect with listeners on a personal level.

About Tanmay

Tanmay Hardia developed a passion for music and songwriting from a young age. Inspired by emotions, thoughts, and real-life experiences, he writes songs that express feelings through poetic lyrics and heartfelt melodies.

As an independent artist, Tanmay continues to explore new musical ideas and create original songs while building his journey as a professional singer and songwriter.

Music & Creativity

For Tanmay, music is a powerful way to express emotions and ideas. Through songwriting and singing, he transforms thoughts and experiences into songs that people can feel and relate to.
